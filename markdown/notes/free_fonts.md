There are so many wonderful free fonts in the world. The below are my favorites. They are all so good I sort-of can’t believe they’re free. Truly, we live in a wondrous world.

- [Brill](https://brill.com/page/BrillFont/brill-typeface)
- [Charter](https://practicaltypography.com/charter.html) (and [XCharter](https://ctan.org/tex-archive/fonts/xcharter))
- [Classico](https://ctan.org/tex-archive/fonts/urw/classico), a clone of Optima by the original designer
- [EB Garamond](https://googlefonts.github.io/ebgaramond-specimen/)
- [IBM Plex Family](https://www.ibm.com/plex/)
- [Inter](https://rsms.me/inter/)
- [JetBrains Mono](https://www.jetbrains.com/lp/mono/)
- [Libre Franklin](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Libre+Franklin)
- [Libre Baskerville](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Libre+Baskerville?preview.script=Latn)
- [Monaspace](https://monaspace.githubnext.com)
- [P052 (Palladio)](https://github.com/ArtifexSoftware/urw-base35-fonts), a clone of Palatino by the original designer
- [Roboto Flex](https://m3.material.io/blog/roboto-flex)
- [Source Serif 4](http://adobe-fonts.github.io/source-serif/)
- [Utopia](https://ghostscript.com/~tor/stuff/fonts/utopia/) ([info](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utopia_(typeface))) also: [Erewhon](https://ctan.org/tex-archive/fonts/erewhon)

Some noteable [fonts included in macOS Tahoe](https://support.apple.com/en-us/122869) (You'll have to explicitly [download](https://support.apple.com/guide/font-book/install-and-validate-fonts-fntbk1000/mac) some of these):

- [Brill](https://brill.com/page/BrillFont/brill-typeface)
- [Canela](https://commercialtype.com/catalog/canela)
- [Charter](https://practicaltypography.com/charter.html)
- [Founders Grotesk](https://klim.co.nz/retail-fonts/founders-grotesk/)
- [Graphik](https://commercialtype.com/catalog/graphik)
- [Optima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optima)
- [Produkt](https://commercialtype.com/catalog/produkt)
- [Publico](https://commercialtype.com/catalog/publico)
